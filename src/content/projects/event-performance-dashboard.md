_Data Analytics Internship at Eskwelabs, February to May 2026._

## The problem

Event results were spread across four years of records and reported by hand, so there was no single place for stakeholders to evaluate revenue.

## What I built

- An automated Looker Studio dashboard consolidating **80+ events and 1,000+ attendees** into one view
- Masked datasets to protect sensitive information while testing the pipeline logic before launch

## Result

Manual reporting was replaced with a dashboard stakeholders can open any time to evaluate event revenue.
