_Team Lead and Backend Engineer, Solar Power Hackathon, August 2025._

## The idea

Air quality readings alone don't tell a person how much pollution *they* are exposed to. AeroC turns hourly PM2.5 readings into personalized exposure risk estimates for the Philippines and Indonesia.

## What I did

- Led the backend team
- Built a Python and FastAPI service that ingests hourly PM2.5 readings
- Built aggregation and risk-scoring pipelines for the time-series data
- Deployed it on AWS (EC2, S3)

## Result

**1st place out of 25 teams.**
