## What happened

Our team built **AeroC**, an air pollution exposure simulator that turns hourly PM2.5 readings into personalized exposure risk estimates for the Philippines and Indonesia. We placed **1st out of 25 teams**.

I led the backend team and built the Python and FastAPI service, deployed on AWS. The full write-up is on the [AeroC project page](/projects/aeroc/).
