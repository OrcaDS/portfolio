## What happened

I ran [Beat the Bot](/projects/beat-the-bot/) live at a booth, where visitors played against a bot that predicts their next choice and adapts as they play.

- **41 participants**
- **436 decisions**
- **51 completed games**

A diagnostic dashboard tracked choice distributions, accuracy trends and outcomes during the event, with PDF reports afterwards.
